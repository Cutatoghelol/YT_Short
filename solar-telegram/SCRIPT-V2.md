# The Sun Sent a Telegram — V2

The spoken script and speaker assignments are in [dialogue.json](src/v2/dialogue.json).

The Boston–Portland exchange is paraphrased from the operators’ account. The equipment, room layouts, and gestures are dramatized. The burning paper is a separate visual reenactment of effects reported during the storm. [Prescott's account](https://ajsonline.org/api/v1/articles/66165-the-great-auroral-exhibition-of-august-28th-to-september-4th-1859.pdf) · [Contemporary accounts of sparks and burning paper](https://ajsonline.org/api/v1/articles/64677-on-the-great-auroral-exhibition-of-aug-28th-to-sept-4th-1859-and-on-auroras-generally-8th-article.pdf)

The cross-section illustrates the electric field induced at ground level and the current it drove in a grounded long line. The aurora and the telegraph interference share a cause in the geomagnetic storm. [USGS](https://www.usgs.gov/programs/geomagnetism/science/geomagnetically-induced-currents)
