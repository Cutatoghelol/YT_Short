# The Sun Sent a Telegram — V4

The [spoken script](src/v4/dialogue.json) dramatizes the exchange that George B. Prescott reported from September 2, 1859. The Boston and Portland operators disconnected their batteries, grounded the line, and kept exchanging telegrams for roughly two hours. The chair incident and operators' reactions are dramatized. The paper fire depicts a separate reported incident during the storm. [Prescott’s account](https://ajsonline.org/api/v1/articles/66165-the-great-auroral-exhibition-of-august-28th-to-september-4th-1859.pdf) · [Loomis’s collected reports, pp. 322–323](https://ajsonline.org/article/64677.pdf)

The underground shot illustrates how a changing magnetic field can induce a geoelectric field at ground level that drives current through long grounded lines. The aurora depicts another effect of the storm. [USGS explanation](https://www.usgs.gov/programs/geomagnetism/science/geomagnetically-induced-currents)
